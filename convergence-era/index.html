Thought Leadership · 2026

The Museum in the Convergence Era.

Industrial design, AI, and the transformation of cultural systems.

Spatial computing, embodied AI, and distributed intelligence are not future concerns. They are design decisions being made right now, in master plans and feasibility studies.

Museums are shifting from static interpretive spaces to responsive, perceptual environments that can change content, lighting, narrative, and pathways based on how people move and interact.

Museum Planning LLC works at this intersection, translating the framework of Convergence Era (2026) into built, operational reality. It is the theoretical foundation for Museums 101, Second Edition, and the Culture Everywhere platform.
