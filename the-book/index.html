The Book

Designing Museum Experiences.

A how-to book for creating visitor-centered museums that connect emotionally and intellectually with visitors, stakeholders, and donors.

Bloomsbury · 2021
Designing MuseumExperiences
Mark Walhimer
museum-experiences.com
Buy on Amazon →

Designing Museum Experiences leads readers through the methods and tools of the three stages of a museum visit — pre-visit, in-person visit, and post-visit — with the goal of motivating visitors to return and revisit the museum in the future.

Museums are changing from static, encyclopedic institutions to visitor-centric organizations with shared authority, where museum and visitors become co-creators of content. The book gives museum teams the tools to make that change: personas, journey mapping, empathy mapping, and more. It also addresses the challenges museums face in reopening after COVID-19.

Inside the book.

  1. The Visitor-Centered Museum. From institution-first to visitor-first. Empathy, shared authority, and co-creation.
  2. The Three Stages of a Museum Visit. Pre-visit, in-person, and post-visit. Designing each touchpoint with intent.
  3. Tools for Understanding Visitors. Empathy mapping, personas, audience segmentation, and visitor journey mapping.
  4. Designing the Experience. Service design blueprints, system mapping, content mapping, and stakeholder mapping.
  5. The Visitor Value Proposition. Creating meaningful intellectual, emotional, and experiential value. The visitation loop.
