The Book

A how-to guide for every museum professional.

Designing Museum Experiences leads readers through the methods and tools of the three stages of a museum visit — pre-visit, in-person visit, and post-visit — with the goal of motivating visitors to return.

Museums are changing from static, encyclopedic institutions to visitor-centric organizations with shared authority, where museum and visitors co-create content. This book makes that transformation actionable.
